How SmazhPerkPoint works

No forms to fill in online and nothing to carry — your phone number is your membership. Three steps and you're earning.

01

Join in store

Ask any Smazh Premium Outlet team member to sign you up at the counter. It takes about a minute — you'll get your own member code, and a welcome email is created for you with your membership details.

02

Shop & earn points

Give your phone number or member code at checkout. You earn 1 point per $1 spent, multiplied by your tier — up to 2× at Diamond. After every shop, a points email is created showing how many points you added and your new total, ready to send to the email on your membership.

03

Spend points on rewards

Trade spendable points for rewards from the member catalog — discount vouchers, gifts and member-only treats. Show your voucher at checkout and the discount is applied.

Questions, answered

How do I join SmazhPerkPoint?

Joining happens in store at Smazh Premium Outlet — just ask any team member at the counter. They'll register you with your name, phone number and email, and a welcome email with your member code and starting tier is created for you. It takes about a minute.

How do I earn points when I shop?

Simply give your phone number or member code at checkout. You earn 1 point per $1 spent, multiplied by your tier — 1× at Bronze up to 2× at Diamond. Points are added by the team member serving you, and a points email is created showing how many points were added and your new total balance.

Can my tier go down if I spend my points?

No. Your tier is based on lifetime points — the total you have ever earned — and that number never decreases. Spending points on rewards only lowers your spendable balance, never your tier.

How do I redeem a reward?

Ask the team about the current rewards catalog in store, choose your reward, and the points are deducted from your spendable balance. Discount rewards give you a voucher to show at checkout.

How do I check my points balance?

Every time points are added, a points email is created for you with the points added and your updated total. You can also ask any team member in store to look up your balance with your phone number or member code.

I don't have an email address. Can I still join?

Yes — you can still be registered and earn points with your phone number. An email address is needed to receive the welcome email and points updates, so add one whenever you're ready and the team will update your membership.
