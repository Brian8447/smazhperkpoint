How your tier is decided

Three simple rules, and no annual reset to worry about.

01

Points add up, forever

Every shop adds to your lifetime points — the total you have ever earned since you joined.

02

Spending never costs you a tier

Trading points for rewards lowers your spendable balance only. Lifetime points, and therefore your tier, stay exactly where they are.

03

Checked after every shop

Your points email tells you the points just added and your new total — and the moment that total crosses a threshold, the higher rate applies from the next shop on.

Do I need to spend a certain amount each year?

No. SmazhPerkPoint has no annual spend requirement and no yearly reset. Your tier follows your lifetime points, so a quiet year never takes anything away.

When does the higher earning rate start?

As soon as your lifetime total passes the threshold. Points from the shop that took you over are earned at your old rate; every shop after that earns at the new one.

Can I move up more than one tier at once?

Yes. A large shop can carry you past more than one threshold, and your tier is set by your total — not by how many visits it took.
