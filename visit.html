Four short steps, all of them at the counter.

  1. Visit Smazh Premium OutletCome in for your normal shop — no appointment needed.
  2. Ask to join SmazhPerkPointTell the team member you'd like to become a member before you pay.
  3. Give your name, phone & emailYou'll get your own member code, and a welcome email is created for you.
  4. Start earning straight awayPoints from that first shop land in your account, with a points email showing your new total.

Finding the membership counter

Membership is handled at the outlet's main counter. Ask any team member for the SmazhPerkPoint desk and they'll point you there — signing up takes about a minute.

Checking your points

Every shop creates a points email with the points added and your new total. In store, give your phone number or member code to any team member for a balance check.
